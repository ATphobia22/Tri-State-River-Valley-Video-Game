# Director shot sequence (v1.6)

| Shot | Setting | Grade | Duration | Dramatic purpose |
|---|---|---|---:|---|
| 01 | Ohio Riverfront | Natural | 4.2 s | Establish place and calm water |
| 02 | Valley Farm | Golden | 4.2 s | Agriculture and family warmth |
| 03 | Oak Grove | Lantern | 4.2 s | Night firefly magic and woodland focus |
| 04 | Ohio Riverfront | Storm | 4.2 s | Weather contrast and stylized rain |
| 05 | Ohio Riverfront | Blue | 4.2 s | Twilight closing image |

A looping guided sequence updates the existing lighting look and weather state. Controls allow stepping, pause/resume, a single PNG postcard, and a 5-panel PNG contact sheet generated client-side without external libraries or network.

Production note: these are Canvas 2D concept illustrations. Engine-based dolly rigs, camera motion paths, compositing passes and color-managed native output still require dedicated GPU/engine validation.
